    Brodmann Atlas

    An interactive study atlas of the human cerebral cortex, after Korbinian Brodmann's map of 1909.

    Made by Alessandro Saccoia, Iside Systems, Milan, 2026.

    Surfaces and MRI are the FreeSurfer fsaverage average brain. Area boundaries come from the PALS-B12 Brodmann annotation (Van Essen, 2005), gyri from the Desikan–Killiany atlas (2006). Plates are from Brodmann, Vergleichende Lokalisationslehre der Grosshirnrinde, 1909, via Wikimedia Commons.

    The boundaries are a group average projected on an average brain. Brodmann's areas are defined by cytoarchitecture and vary from one brain to the next. Areas 12, 34 and 52 are missing from the surface, the insula is not subdivided, and some cortex around the lateral sulcus carries no area. The descriptions are study summaries, not a clinical reference.
